In July 2021, I was invited alongside my colleague Christina Hillig to give a talk about "Biocomputational analysis of big data" at the EADV ESDR Summer Research Workshop hosted by [Kilian Eyerich](https://www.med.uni-freiburg.de/de/fakultaet/profs/Eyerich). This workshop provided an excellent opportunity to connect with scientists from diverse backgrounds — medicine, biology, and computer science — all focused on inflammatory skin disease research.
