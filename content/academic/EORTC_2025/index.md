In October 2025, my main PhD project on distinguishing early mycosis fungoides (MF), a rare tumor disease, from benign inflammatory skin disease earned a poster walk presentation at EORTC CLTG in Athens.

In this work, we have identified the two genes _LCK_ and _HOMER1_ as diagnostic biomarkers that enable the early detection of MF and consequently allow the proper treatment of these patients. We have achieved a sensitivity and specificity of 91% and 94% in a validation cohort, respectively. Furthermore, we have tested our developed classifier on 7 international independent multi center cohorts.

This work is now being leveraged by Dermagnostix GmbH in the research-use-only diagnostic test **Erysk**. The preprint containing more information about the two markers and the experiments is available [here](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.10.18.25338021v1).
