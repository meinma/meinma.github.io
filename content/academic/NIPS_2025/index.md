My work on "FocusMR: An Attention-based Single-cell Mendelian Randomization Framework to map cellular contexts at candidate genes" was selected for presentation at the NeurIPS workshop ["AI Virtual Cells and Instruments: A New Era in Drug Discovery and Development"](https://ai4d3.github.io/2025/index.html) in San Diego.

I presented how we use single-cell genetics data and leverage multiple instance learning to identify disease-relevant cell states beyond cell type-level to improve disease understanding and enable precise targeting of candidate genes.
 
This workshop was the highlight end of the NeurIPS week with fascinating speakers such as Eric Xing, Alex Zhavoronkov, Linda Goodman, and Arvind Ramanathan.
Their talks, ranging from automated labs, virtual cells to drug discovery were truly insightful. As a PhD candidate in computational biology, it was interesting to learn how startups work to develop target candidates and where AI can best contribute to accelerate this process.
